Welcome to Eat N Stay

A quiet retreat on Main Road.

Set within Dada Complex, opposite the bustling Mondha market, Hotel Eat N Stay offers a serene escape in the middle of Ambajogai. Our doors open to travellers, families, and pilgrims visiting the historic Yogeshwari Temple.

Every room is designed to feel like a gentle pause — crisp linens, reliable air conditioning, hot water on demand, and a warm smile at the front desk. Our in-house restaurant serves freshly cooked Maharashtrian and North Indian favourites.
